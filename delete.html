Gym Log

Delete your account and data

Fastest: in the app
  1. Open Gym Log and tap the menu (☰).
  2. Open Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Everything is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email gymlog.contact@example.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Gym Log account”. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted: your sign-in, every logged set and workout, workout days and exercises, settings, body weight and goal, your shared plan code, and your group memberships, best sets and ranks. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from AI-answer reports you filed (which contain no account details beyond an anonymous user ID) and backups that Google keeps for a short time.
