Gym Log

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Gym Log is a workout-logging app made by Giorgos Therapontos in Cyprus. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how you can delete it. Contact: gymlog.contact@example.com.

What we store

  • Your account: your sign-in (Google, email and password, or a guest account), your email address and display name.
  • Your training log: workout days, exercises, every set you log (weight, reps, notes and date), your settings and the muscle each exercise counts for.
  • Body weight: weigh-ins and your goal weight, if you enter them.
  • Groups (only if you join one): the name you choose for the group, your best set on exercises the group votes in, your votes and your muscle ranks.
  • Shared plans (only if you create a plan code): your workout days and exercises under that code.
  • Reports: if you report an AI coach answer, the answer, your question and the reason.

Everything is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), linked to your account. Some data is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Who can see it

Only you. If you join a group, its members can see only the name you chose, your best sets on voted-in exercises, your votes and your muscle ranks. Anyone who enters your plan code can copy your workout days and exercise names (not your sets). We never sell your data and there are no ads.

AI coach

When you ask the coach a question, the question and the recent messages of that chat are sent to Google's Gemini AI (through Firebase AI Logic) to write the answer. Chats aren't saved to your account. Please don't share personal health details. AI answers can be wrong and aren't medical advice. To prevent abuse, the coach uses Google reCAPTCHA / App Check, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a set, an exercise or your account removes it from our database.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your log, settings, body weight, plan code, group memberships and your sign-in. You can also request deletion here.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export (Settings → Export to Excel) and delete your data, and object to how it's used. Contact us at the address above. You can also complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

Children

Gym Log isn't meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
